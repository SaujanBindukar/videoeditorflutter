# video_editing_app

Video Editing App
A cross-platform video editing app built with Flutter. Trim videos, add draggable text overlays, and export your creations with custom fonts—powered by FFmpeg and Flutter.

## Features

- 📹 Video trimming with intuitive UI
- 📝 Add multiple draggable text overlays
- 🖋️ Custom font support for overlays (Roboto by default)
- ⚡ Fast export with FFmpeg (only trimmed segment is exported)
- 💾 Save exported videos directly to your device gallery
- 🟢 Works on Android and iOS (best tested on real devices)

## Usage
- Pick a video from your device.
- Trim the video to your desired segment.
- Add and drag text overlays anywhere on the video.
- Tap Export to save the trimmed, annotated video to your gallery.
